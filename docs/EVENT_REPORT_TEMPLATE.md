## 1. Executive Summary

During National Cyber Security Awareness Month (NCSAM) 2026, the School of Cyber Security and Digital Forensics (SCSDF) at NFSU Gandhinagar conducted an interactive cybersecurity awareness activity titled **Lucky Draw**.

The activity simulated the user experience of a suspicious online promotional campaign to teach students about phishing tactics, QR-code risks, browser permissions, and personal data exposure.
